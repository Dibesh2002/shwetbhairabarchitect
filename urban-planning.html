Planning for towns, neighbourhoods and public spaces

Shwet Bhairab Architects & Associates prepares physical development plans, master plans and urban design proposals for communities, local governments and institutions. Our team brings architects, engineers, a sociologist and a sustainability consultant together, so a plan considers land use, infrastructure, social needs and the environment at the same time.

सहरी योजना (sahari yojana) and गुरुयोजना (master plan Nepal) for Kathmandu Valley and the rest of Nepal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a physical development plan include?

A physical development plan typically covers land use zoning, road network planning, public service infrastructure and environmental management. It guides sustainable growth and helps improve living standards in the area.

Do you prepare master plans for schools and parks?

Yes. We have prepared master plans for a secondary school in Bhaktapur and for Palthani Kamla Pokhari Park, and designed a park in Chitwan.

Do you combine urban planning with heritage conservation?

Yes. In historic settlements we plan land use, public space and movement together with conservation of the existing buildings. See our heritage conservation page for examples.
